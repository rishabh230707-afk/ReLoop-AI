# ReLoop demo impact methodology

## Reused mass
Estimated reused mass equals the transferred quantity multiplied by each item's sample unit mass. This estimates material kept in use. It does not establish landfill diversion or carbon emissions avoided. All demo weights are synthetic.

## Financial estimates
Estimated net savings equal quantity multiplied by new-purchase benchmark minus refurbishment per unit, less total transport cost. A benchmark of zero means no purchase replacement value was entered. Financial savings only represent avoided expenditure if a new purchase would otherwise occur. Costs may make the estimate negative.

## Education and partnerships
School units supplied count equipment deliveries, not learning improvements. Active recipient institutions count partners served, not formal agreements. SDG 12 is primary; SDG 4 and SDG 17 are supporting intended contributions. No learning outcomes or carbon reductions have been measured.

## Pilot evaluation
A proposed pilot targets ten accepted transfers between a campus and a consenting school. Follow up after 30 and 90 days to verify continued use. Check purchases actually avoided, repair costs and collection effort before claiming environmental or financial benefit.
