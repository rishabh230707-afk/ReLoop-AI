# ReLoop demo reuse rules

## Partner approval
Only approved demo partners can receive resources. CAMPUS01 is the approved campus and SCHOOL01 is the approved school. SCHOOL02 is awaiting review and cannot receive transfers. These are fictional approval fixtures, not verified institutional agreements.

## School sharing
School requests can only match listings marked school_shareable. Internal-only laboratory equipment cannot be proposed to a school. Available quantity, recipient-specific collection distance and the user's repair preference constrain every match.

## Inspection and transfer
A human must inspect condition, specifications and ownership permission before a transfer. For a monitor, check connectors, power supply and electrical safety with qualified staff. The assistant cannot certify safety or approve a transfer. A user records an inspected demo transfer through the existing review form.

## Excluded items
Food, medicines, chemicals, loose batteries and hazardous materials are outside this prototype's scope. Do not suggest bypassing these exclusions or infer suitability from text similarity.
